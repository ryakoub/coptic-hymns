Features

Made for learning and for the church.

Follow every word

Each word lights up as the cantor sings it, and the hymn scrolls along. Touch a line to hear it from its start.

Repeat and slow down

Repeat the line being sung until you have it, step from line to line, and slow the cantor down to half speed.

Coptic, English and Arabic

The Coptic beside its English, with the Arabic and a transliteration in Latin letters when you want them. English versions sung as English.

Many tunes and cantors

Hear a hymn by more than one cantor, in its annual and festal tunes, and in English where it is sung in English.

Today in the church year

The Coptic date, the season we are in, and the feasts and fasts coming up, each opening its hymns.

Scores that follow along

Hymn scores and hand-written notation, with a cursor that moves across the page as the recording plays.

Offline

Download a whole season before church or a trip, and every hymn plays without a connection.

Find the line you remember

Search titles and the words themselves, in English, Coptic or Arabic.

My hymns

Add your own recordings and notation, mark them as you listen, and back them up or share them with your class.

Made for the pew

Large text that fits the screen, the two sides of the choir tinted apart, a traditional Coptic font, and a screen that stays on.

Share a hymn

Send a hymn to a friend or a class: the link opens it in the app, or here on the website.

Always growing

New hymns, words and timings arrive by themselves, without waiting for an app update.

Serve with us

Help time the hymns.

Every hymn is timed by hand, word by word, by volunteers using the app’s pencil. If you can spare an hour, write to hello@copticchanter.com and we’ll send you a hymn to time.
