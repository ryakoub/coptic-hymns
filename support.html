Support

Help with the Coptic Chanter app for iPhone and iPad.

Get in touch

For a question, a wrong word, a recording that will not play or anything else, send us a message. Please name the hymn and the recording, and say what you expected to see.

Testing the app through TestFlight? Take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback. It reaches us with the details we need.

Common questions

Can I use the app without the internet?

Yes, once the hymns are on your device. Open a season or program in the library and tap the download button at the top to keep all its recordings for offline use.

Why don't the words light up in some hymns?

The words light up as they are sung only in recordings that have been timed. A hymn marked Not timed yet plays with its words, but without the highlighting. Volunteers are timing more hymns, and they reach the app without an update.

How do I show only Coptic, only English, or the transliteration?

Open Settings (the gear button) and choose under Languages. The text size, the Coptic font, and the transliteration above or in place of the Coptic are there too.

Why does the screen stay on?

So it doesn't dim while you follow a hymn. You can turn this off in Settings: Keep the screen on while a hymn is open.

What is My hymns?

A private place for your own slides or scores and recordings, with a cursor that follows the recording. Everything in My hymns stays on your device. To move it to another device, use Options › Back up and open the backup there.

Where do the hymns come from?

The words, translations, recordings and scores are credited in the app under Settings › About & Credits.
