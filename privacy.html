Privacy

Coptic Chanter collects no personal information.

Last updated October 26, 2026

Coptic Chanter is a free app for iPhone and iPad, and this website, offered by the Coptic Orthodox Churches of Chicagoland and the Midwest to help people learn and follow the hymns of the Church. This page explains what happens to your information when you use them.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone, and it does not send anything about you or how you use it to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, such as languages, text size and appearance.
  • Hymns and recordings you download to use offline.
  • My hymns: slides, recordings and marks you add yourself. They stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Word timings you make in the app, until you choose to export them.

Deleting the app deletes all of these. A backup or an exported file goes only where you choose to send it, for example to Files, AirDrop or a message.

What the app downloads

The app downloads the hymn list, words, translations, timings, recordings and music scores from copticchanter.com. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep the internet address and time of each request in its server logs for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs or use them to identify anyone.

Some hymns link to other websites, such as YouTube, Tasbeha.org or Coptic Hymns in English. Those open in your browser, under those websites' own privacy policies.

Crash reports and test feedback

If you allow it in your device's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. If you test the app through TestFlight and choose to send feedback or a screenshot, Apple passes it on to us with the email address of your TestFlight account. We use it only to fix the app.

The contact form

If you write to us through the contact page, the name, email or phone number and message you enter are sent to us through Formspree, a form service, and used only to reply to you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the contact page.
